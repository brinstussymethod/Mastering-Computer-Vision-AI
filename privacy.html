Privacy Policy

Club Ops Agent · Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Club Ops Agent program ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you receive SMS text-message reminders from us. By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to the practices described below.

Our commitment: We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for marketing or promotional purposes. Your data is used only to operate the reminder service you signed up for.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to send you club reminders:

  • Your name — so reminders can be addressed and routed to the right member.
  • Your mobile phone number — so we can deliver SMS reminders.
  • Task and event details — the club tasks, deadlines, and event information relevant to you (drawn from the club's own calendar and meeting notes).
  • Message-related metadata — basic delivery records such as send time and delivery status, used to confirm reminders went through.

We do not collect financial information, government identifiers, location data, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Send you SMS reminders about club tasks, deadlines, and upcoming events.
  • Confirm whether reminders were delivered and follow up on outstanding tasks.
  • Respond to your HELP requests and honor your STOP opt-out requests.

3. We Do Not Share Your Information

We will never share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information (including your phone number) with any third party for their own use, and we will never use your information for marketing or promotional purposes.

The only third party involved is our SMS delivery provider, Twilio, which transmits the text messages on our behalf. Twilio processes this data solely to deliver messages and is bound by its own privacy commitments. No mobile information is shared with any other third party, and no data is shared for marketing or advertising.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only for as long as you are an active member of the club's reminder list. If you opt out (reply STOP) or leave the club, your number is removed from the active list. You may request deletion of your information at any time using the contact details below.

5. Data Security

Access to member contact information is restricted to the club operator. Messages are sent over Twilio's secure, encrypted infrastructure. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Your Choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe and stop all messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly (below).
  • Contact us to review, update, or delete the information we hold about you.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is intended for club members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued participation after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
